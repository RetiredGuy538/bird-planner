Scoring guide

What's New in v5.0

  • 🔭 eBird recent sightings added — Jefferson County WI, last 7 days, filtered against Haikubox species
  • 🎭 Blind Setup Windows now only shown when 8+ consecutive hours of eff. wind ≤5 mph
  • 📅 7-day forecast headers cleaned up — solunar info removed (available in hourly detail)
  • Previous: Session Length replaced by Actual Wind + Effective Wind in pick cards

Wind Logic

  • Deck faces west over Lake Koshkonong
  • Pure W wind <5 mph: no penalty — birds still present
  • Pure W wind 5–10 mph: ⚠️ −15 pts — birds may move to far shore
  • Pure W wind >10 mph: 🚫 score capped at 50 — birds likely gone
  • Easterly (E, NE, ENE): shielded ~70% — effective wind much lower
  • ⛨ = sheltered (easterly wind direction)

Solunar Science

  • 🌕 Major periods: moon overhead or underfoot (±1 hr) — peak feeding
  • 🌙 Minor periods: moonrise / moonset (±1 hr) — moderate feeding
  • Aligned with golden hour = 🌕 Prime Session designation
  • Calculated entirely from lunar position math — no external API

Score Breakdown

  • 🌕 Prime: golden hour + calm + major solunar — rarest, best sessions
  • 75–100 Excellent — set up blind tonight
  • 55–74 Good — worth setting up same morning
  • 35–54 Fair — casual observation only
  • Wind 40 + Light 40 + Sun 30 + Dry 10 + Solunar 20 → scaled to /100
  • Pure W wind penalty applied after normalization
